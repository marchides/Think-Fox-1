How Fox Four uses the shelf
Text documents (up to 5 MiB each) are split into overlapping passages and ranked locally using BM25, title and phrase matches, with overlap suppression. This lexical search uses no remote embeddings. PDF, DOCX and images must first be converted to text. Pin a book to include a passage even without a keyword match. Disabled and unselected books are excluded. Global books are available everywhere; project books are available only to their assigned project.
